Product images need to meet Amazon's technical requirements — background, framing, sizing — while representing the product exactly as it is. That second part matters as much as the first, which is why product accuracy is a firm, non-negotiable rule in how we work on this service (see the note on this page).

The result is an image set that looks professional and meets marketplace requirements without misrepresenting the product to a customer.
